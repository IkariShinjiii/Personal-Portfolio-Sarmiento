## Multimedia Systems course
Kervin takes IT 312 Multimedia Systems in the BSIT program at the University of San Agustin. The course covers how sound, images and video are captured, stored, compressed and delivered: digital audio and video, color models (RGB, YCbCr), lossless coding (run-length, Huffman, LZW, arithmetic), lossy compression (JPEG, MPEG), and multimedia authoring with storyboards. His main takeaway is that every media format trades size against quality and speed, and it changed how he chooses formats and compression for web work. Course work on the site, each with its own page at https://sarmiento-portfolio-multimedia.vercel.app/multimedia/<name>: Research Infographic (research-infographic), Silent Movie Storyboard (silent-movie-storyboard), Silent Movie Video (silent-movie-video) and Stop Animation (stop-animation). Some of the files are still being added.
